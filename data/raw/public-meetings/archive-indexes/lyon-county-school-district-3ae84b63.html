Portrait of a Learner

Our Guiding Compass

The Guiding Compass for the Lyon County School District Learning Experience

The Portrait of a Learner embodies the learning journey in the Lyon CSD through four domains and multiple competencies. The District motto of “Every Student… Every Classroom… Every Day” comes to life when you step into our schools and see students engaged in rigorous, real-life learning opportunities within the domains of:

Learning For Life

Connected Learners

Student Ownership

Discovery Learning
