Storey County News

  1. Storey County Offers Mailed Garbage Vouchers for Residents

    Storey County is now offering residents the option to have their annual garbage vouchers mailed directly to them, providing a more convenient and time-saving way to access this service. Read on...

  2. Sheriff's Night Out in Lockwood & Virginia City

    Mark your calendars! Sheriff’s Night Out is returning to Lockwood on Aug. 25 and Virginia City on Sept. 1 for another evening of food, fun, and community connection. Read on...

  3. Community Project Grant Applications Now Open!

    Got a great idea for your community? This year, Storey County has doubled the funding for the Community Project Grant Program. Community groups can now apply for up to $20,000 per area to bring projects that benefit local residents to life! Read on...
