Mission:

Lincoln County School District ensures every student learns at high levels by maintaining clear communication, consistent instructional practices, and accountability for student success.

Welcome to Lincoln County School District

Welcome to the Lincoln County School District! As your Superintendent, I am committed to leading with Communication, Consistency, and Accountability at the forefront of all we do. These values guide every decision we make, ensuring thatour students, staff, families, and community members feel informed, supported, and valued.

Our mission is simple yet powerful: to prepare every student for success in life—whatever path they choose after school. Whether their journey leads to college, career, the military, or other opportunities, we are dedicated to equipping them with the skills, knowledge, and character to thrive. I have deep roots in this community—I grew up here, attended these schools, and know firsthand the pride and strength of Lincoln County. My love for this community drives my work every day. I believe our youth are our most important investment, and I am committed to doing what is best for them. We know that student success is a shared effort—it is built not only in our classrooms but also through strong support at home and in the community. Together, we can create the best possible environment for learning, growth, and opportunity.

Thank you for trusting us with your children’s education. I look forward to working alongside you to ensure Lincoln County School District remains a place where students can dream big, work hard, and achieve their goals.

Matt Cameron Superintendent
