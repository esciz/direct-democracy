Mission & Philosophy

About Us

The mission of Lander County School District, in collaboration with parents and the community, is to provide the environment and resources to enable students to be prepared, educated, and confident in their ability to take charge of their future and positively contribute to their community.

About LCSD

Lander County School District is looking for a School Bus Driver. 

Position: School Bus Route Driver
Start Date: Upcoming School Year

Compensation:

  • Hourly Rate for Routes: $22.47 – $37.16/hr

  • Trip Pay:

    • "Down" Time: $20/hr

    • Mileage: 70¢ per mile

Apply Today!
Interested applicants should submit a formal letter of interest, resume, and completed application to hr@landernv.net - contact: Cassandra Anson. 

Help us ensure a safe and successful school year for our students, become a valued part of our team!
