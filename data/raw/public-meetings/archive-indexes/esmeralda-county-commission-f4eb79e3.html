OUT-OF-SERVICE VEHICLE AUCTION

The Esmeralda County Fire District is pleased to announce the liquidation of the following out-of-service Vehicles (Vehicle List Link ). All vehicles listed will be auctioned by sealed bid closing at 5:00pm PST on September 30th, 2026 to the highest most responsive bidder.

Submit bids using Bid Form “BID FORM Link” by US Mail to Esmeralda County Fire District P.O. Box 12 Goldfield, NV 89013 to via email to jbushnell@esmeraldacountynv.gov . 

For additional information, or to look at in person prior to closing please contact
Chief Bushnell at (775) 745-3647 or via email at jbushnell@esmeraldacountynv.gov



On Tuesday, April 7, 2026, Esmeralda County received a shelter trailer from the American Red Cross in coordination with the Esmeralda County Fire District. The trailer is stocked with essential supplies to support shelter operations if we need to open a shelter within the county during an emergency. It is also available to assist neighboring counties as part of regional mutual aid when shelter support is needed.  Photo to follow.


NOTICE OF REQUEST FOR A COMMUNITY MEMBER OR THE LIQOUR BOARD:  Please send an email or letter to the Board of County Commission Office if you are interested in being the community member of the Liquor Board.  Email to bocc@esmeraldacountynv.gov or by mail to PO Box 517, Goldfield, NV  89013.  For questions, please call 775-485-3406. 

ABOUT US:

Esmeralda County, Nevada is home to many historic sites, mining communities, ghost towns and a lush farming community. Located essentially half way between Las Vegas and Reno on Highway 95, it is surrounded by scenic mesas, rugged mountain wilderness and vast open basins. Esmeralda County offers a variety of spectacular scenery, a comfortable lifestyle, outstanding recreational activities as well as unlimited business opportunities.  Take the time to visit Esmeralda County today or make plans for a visit and bring friends.  Check out the Tourism page and where to go exploring in Esmeralda County.
