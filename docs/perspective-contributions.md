# Perspective contributions

The Challenge My View explorer accepts community-proposed statements through a moderated contribution workflow.

## Eligibility

- Verified citizens can submit.
- Trusted citizens can submit and their trusted status is visible to reviewers.
- Trusted status never bypasses review.
- Unverified and signed-out visitors can explore approved perspectives but cannot submit.

## Required submission shape

Contributors provide a policy-focused statement, context, the strongest case for it, the strongest challenge, shared ground, evidence to test, affected people, possible policy paths, and at least one public source URL.

Statements should challenge a policy, rule, institution, or public claim. They should not frame a racial, religious, immigrant, LGBTQ, disabled, or other identity group as the problem.

## Review and publication

Every submission starts as `pending` in the private perspective-review queue. An administrator with review approval permission must approve or reject it. Only approved records are transformed into public explorer topics.

Reviewers should check:

- whether each side would recognize its argument as fair;
- whether the framing avoids stereotypes and collective blame;
- whether sources are credible and relevant;
- whether evidence questions could genuinely change the discussion;
- whether affected people and policy alternatives extend beyond two partisan camps.

Production records use the configured private Vercel Blob store. Local development uses `.local/perspective-suggestions`.
